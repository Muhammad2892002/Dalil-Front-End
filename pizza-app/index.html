THE OVEN

Built from Neapolitan brick, fired with oak.

We brought in a mason from Naples to build our oven by hand. It holds heat at 900°F, which means a pizza spends less than two minutes inside — just long enough for the crust to blister and the cheese to stay soft. Everything else follows from that: dough proofed for two full days, toppings kept simple so they don't fight the crust, and a menu short enough that we make every pizza the same way, every time.

The oven does most of the work. We just don't get in its way.
